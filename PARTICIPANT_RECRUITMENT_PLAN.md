# BuzzMap Participant Recruitment Plan

## Analysis of Current Framework vs. BuzzMap Needs

The provided framework shows a general participant identification structure, but BuzzMap requires a more specialized approach for dengue prevention and public health applications.

## Step 1: Identify Participants - BuzzMap Adaptation

### Target Population Analysis

Based on the BuzzMap system requirements and user roles, here's the adapted participant framework:

| Participant Category              | Population Size               | Target Sample Size | Recruitment Strategy                            |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Community Members (End-Users)** | ~2.9M (Quezon City residents) | **12**             | Community centers, social media, health clinics |
| **Health Workers/CHWs**           | ~500 (estimated)              | **5**              | DOH Quezon City, Barangay Health Centers        |
| **Health Administrators**         | ~25 (public health officials) | **3**              | City Health Office, District Health Offices     |
| **IT/Tech Professionals**         | ~50 (in health sector)        | **2**              | Healthcare IT departments, consultants          |
| **Super Administrators**          | ~5 (system admin roles)       | **1**              | Direct contact with development team            |

**Total Target: 23 participants** (exceeds the typical 15-participant recommendation for comprehensive coverage)

## Participant Demographics & Requirements

### 1. Community Members (Primary Focus - 12 participants)

**Demographics:**

- **Age Range**: 18-65 years
- **Education**: High school to college level
- **Tech Literacy**: Basic to intermediate
- **Location**: Various barangays in Quezon City
- **Language**: Filipino/English speakers

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- Residents of Quezon City for at least 1 year
- Have used smartphone/computer for basic tasks
- Familiar with dengue as a health concern
- Willing to participate in 90-minute session

**Recruitment Channels:**

- Barangay health centers
- Community Facebook groups
- Local health awareness events
- University campuses (UP Diliman area)
- Healthcare clinics

**Representation Goals:**

- 6 mobile-primary users, 6 desktop-primary users
- Mix of age groups: 4 (18-30), 4 (31-45), 4 (46-65)
- Various tech comfort levels: 4 low, 4 medium, 4 high
- Geographic spread across different barangays

### 2. Health Workers/Community Health Workers (5 participants)

**Demographics:**

- **Role**: Barangay Health Workers, nurses, health aides
- **Experience**: 1+ years in community health
- **Tech Literacy**: Basic to intermediate
- **Familiarity**: Dengue surveillance and reporting

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- Currently working in Quezon City health system
- Involved in dengue prevention/case reporting
- Experience with digital health tools (preferred)

**Recruitment Channels:**

- Department of Health - Quezon City
- Barangay Health Centers
- Rural Health Units
- Community health worker associations

### 3. Health Administrators (3 participants)

**Demographics:**

- **Role**: Public health officers, epidemiologists, health managers
- **Experience**: 3+ years in public health administration
- **Tech Literacy**: Intermediate to advanced
- **Decision-making**: Authority in health interventions

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- Supervisory role in Quezon City health system
- Experience with health data analysis
- Involvement in dengue outbreak response

**Recruitment Channels:**

- City Health Office direct contact
- Department of Health - NCR
- Philippine Field Epidemiology Training Program
- Public health professional networks

### 4. IT/Tech Professionals (2 participants)

**Demographics:**

- **Role**: Healthcare IT specialists, system administrators
- **Experience**: 2+ years in health technology
- **Tech Literacy**: Advanced
- **Perspective**: Technical usability and system integration

**Inclusion Criteria:**

- Experience with health information systems
- Understanding of public health workflows
- Familiarity with web applications and databases

### 5. Super Administrator (1 participant)

**Demographics:**

- **Role**: System administrator or developer familiar with BuzzMap
- **Experience**: Direct knowledge of BuzzMap architecture
- **Tech Literacy**: Expert level

## Diversity & Inclusion Considerations

### Ensuring Representative Sample

- **Geographic**: Participants from at least 5 different barangays
- **Socioeconomic**: Mix of income levels and educational backgrounds
- **Age**: Balanced representation across age groups
- **Gender**: Aim for 50/50 gender balance
- **Tech Experience**: Range from basic to advanced users
- **Language**: Include both Filipino and English primary speakers

### Accessibility Accommodations

- **Physical**: Accessible testing location
- **Linguistic**: Translator available if needed
- **Technical**: Assistance with technology if required
- **Time**: Flexible scheduling for different work schedules
