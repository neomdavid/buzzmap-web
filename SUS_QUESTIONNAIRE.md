## Scoring Instructions (For Evaluators)

### SUS Score Calculation:

1. For odd-numbered questions (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): Score = (Response - 1)
2. For even-numbered questions (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): Score = (5 - Response)
3. Add up all scores and multiply by 2.5
4. Final SUS Score = Sum × 2.5 (Range: 0-100)

### Interpretation:

- **80-100**: Excellent usability
- **70-79**: Good usability
- **60-69**: Okay usability
- **50-59**: Poor usability
- **0-49**: Awful usability

### Adjective Ratings:

- **84+**: Best Imaginable
- **74-84**: Excellent
- **68-74**: Good
- **52-68**: OK
- **25-52**: Poor
- **0-25**: Worst Imaginable
